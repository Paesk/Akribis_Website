Humanoid robots are becoming affordable and are starting to appear in industrial environments. Companies have already shown humanoids welding in real workshops – but remotely operated and without reported accuracy. Calibration alone improves a humanoid's accuracy to a few millimetres, still far from what welding and other manufacturing tasks demand.

Industry already knows the solution: combine a coarse robot with a fast, precise correction stage at the tool. But these systems are built for large industrial robots – not for the reach, weight and balance of a humanoid.

**That is the gap Akribis addresses.**
